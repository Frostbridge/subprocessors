Updates
Frostbridge may update this Subprocessor List from time to time. Customers that require notice of new subprocessors should email privacy@frostbridge.ai with the subject "Subprocessor Updates" to subscribe, or monitor this page.
Objections
Where required by the Customer Agreement or Data Processing Addendum, Customer may object to a new subprocessor by sending written notice to privacy@frostbridge.ai within the stated objection period.
